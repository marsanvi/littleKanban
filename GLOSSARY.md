# littleKanban

The language for organizing personal and shared work.

## Language

**Project**:
A collection of related tickets, such as a work project or a family holiday.

**Ticket**:
A piece of work or a plan tracked within a project.
_Avoid_: Issue

**Project member**:
A person named within a project who can be responsible for a ticket.

**Assignee**:
The project member responsible for a ticket, if one is assigned.

**Tag**:
A project-specific label used to categorize tickets. A ticket can have
multiple tags.

**Board**:
A visual view of tickets used to organize work. A board can show tickets from
one project or bring together tickets from multiple projects.

**Combined board**:
A personal view of tickets from selected projects.

**Calendar view**:
A view of tickets placed on their due dates, for one project or selected
projects.

**Column**:
A grouping of tickets by their status on a board: To do, In progress, or Done.

**Archived ticket**:
A ticket hidden from normal boards but retained so it can be viewed and restored.
